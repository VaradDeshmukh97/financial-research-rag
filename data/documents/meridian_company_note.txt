MERIDIAN GRID SYSTEMS — SYNTHETIC COMPANY NOTE
Document date: 12 March 2025

Meridian Grid Systems reported FY2024 revenue of USD 88.1 million and operating income of USD 7.9 million. The company supplies monitoring and control equipment to utility and distributed-energy customers.

Management described order conversion timing as a key source of quarterly variability. The note did not disclose customer-level backlog or cancellation data.
