NORTHSTAR COMPONENTS — SYNTHETIC Q1 UPDATE
Document date: 8 May 2025

For the quarter ended 31 March 2025, Northstar Components reported revenue of USD 34.2 million. Management described demand from industrial power systems customers as stable. The quarterly update did not provide a full-year revenue forecast.

Gross margin for the quarter was 32.0%. Management cited product mix as a contributor. The company stated that it had added a second source for selected electronic components, but did not disclose a quantified reduction in supplier concentration.
