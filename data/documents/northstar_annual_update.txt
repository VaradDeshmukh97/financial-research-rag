NORTHSTAR COMPONENTS — SYNTHETIC ANNUAL UPDATE
Document date: 20 February 2025

Northstar Components reported revenue of USD 125.4 million for the year ended 31 December 2024, compared with USD 108.0 million in the prior year. Management attributed the increase primarily to higher shipment volumes in its industrial power systems business and improved pricing on selected product lines.

Gross margin was 31.2%, compared with 29.8% in the prior year. The company attributed the improvement to a more favorable product mix and lower expedited freight costs. These figures are fictional and are included solely to demonstrate retrieval.

The company reported EBITDA of USD 18.6 million for FY2024. Management stated that capacity utilization improved during the second half of the year. The update did not provide a quantified bridge between utilization and EBITDA growth.

Management identified customer concentration and component lead times as operating risks. The five largest customers represented approximately 42% of annual revenue.
